The Green Felt Club was built by people who play pool, not by a software company chasing a vertical. Every choice starts with the same question: does this make the night better for players, organisers, and the room as a whole?

Common Questions

Short answers before you break the next rack

Native FAQ accordions keep the answers accessible, keyboard friendly, and easy to scan on mobile.

Does it support 8-ball and 9-ball?

Yes. The current site messaging and app flow are built around live 8-ball and 9-ball scoring, with stats and history kept from those sessions.

Do all players need accounts?

Sessions can stay fast, but account-backed profiles are the best way to keep long-term stats, synced history, and support options tied to the right player.

Can venues run competitions?

Yes. Venue organisers can request access and use organiser workflows for approvals, competitions, and player join experiences.

Is data synced across devices?

Account-backed match history and stats are stored in the cloud flow so players and organisers can return to the same records later.

What platforms are live now?

The web app is live now. Android is available on Google Play. iOS is now available on the App Store.

What kind of stats does it focus on?

The focus is practical match history: results, player records, and head-to-head views that make recurring matchups easier to track over time.

How much does it cost for venues?

Three tiers, billed annually with a 30-day free trial: Starter $500/year, Pro $1,000/year, and Multi-venue $2,000/year. Monthly billing is also available without a trial. See full pricing.

Do players need to pay?

No. Players are always free. The venue subscription covers everything, so players can open the app and play.
