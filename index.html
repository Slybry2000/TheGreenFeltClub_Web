INDEX / 08   COMMON QUESTIONS

Short answers
before you break
the next rack.

Does it support 8-ball and 9-ball?

Yes. The site messaging and app flow are built around live 8-ball and 9-ball scoring, with stats and history kept from those sessions.

Do all players need accounts?

Sessions can stay fast, but account-backed profiles are the best way to keep long-term stats, synced history, and support options tied to the right player.

Can venues run competitions?

Yes. Venue organisers can request access and use organiser workflows for approvals, competitions, and player join experiences.

Is data synced across devices?

Account-backed match history and stats live in the cloud flow so players and organisers can return to the same records later.

What platforms are live now?

The web app is live now. Android is available on Google Play. iOS is on the App Store.

What kind of stats does it focus on?

Practical match history: results, player records, head-to-head views that make recurring matchups easier to track over time.

How much does it cost for venues?

Three tiers: Starter $1,990/year (1 competition, 50 players), Standard $3,990/year (unlimited competitions and players), and Multi-venue $3,990/year for the first venue + $2,990/year each additional. Annual plans include a 30-day free trial; monthly billing also available on Starter and Standard. Plus a Founding Venue offer at $799/year locked for 3 years — first 5 only, then Charter at $2,388/year for venues 6–10. See full pricing.

Do players need to pay?

No. Players are always free. The venue subscription covers everything, so players can open the app and play.
