Venue + Organizer Intake

Venue Access Request Form

Submit this form to request access for venue and organizer competition tools. After review and approval, you can run in-house competitions, manage scoring modes, set participant caps, and publish live displays.

If you attach a file, the generated email will include the filename so you can attach it before sending.

Submitting opens a prefilled email draft to admin@thegreenfeltclub.com for final send.

Requests are reviewed manually. Access is subject to approval and applicable terms. Introductory no-charge access, if offered, is promotional and may be modified, limited, or discontinued at any time in The Green Felt Club's sole discretion.
