FAQ

Is this just for serious players?

No. It works for casual groups too. The key use case is simply that the same people play often enough to care about real records.

Does it replace live scoring?

No. The stats and history are strongest because they are tied to the same live-scoring flow used during the match.

Want the next rivalry argument settled by the record?

Start with live scoring, then keep the head-to-head history so the stats are there the next time the same players meet.
