Account Deletion

Delete your account and associated data from The Green Felt Club app.

A

Delete via Email Request

✓ No app installation required

You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by sending an email. This method does not require you to have the app installed.

How to Request Deletion:

  1. Send an email to: admin@thegreenfeltclub.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address associated with your account
  4. We will process your request and confirm deletion

Processing Time

Within 7 business days

Confirmation

Email confirmation sent

Send Deletion Request Email
B

Delete Directly in the App

Alternative method if you have the app installed

If you currently have The Green Felt Club app installed, you can also delete your account directly within the app:

In-App Deletion Steps:

  1. Open The Green Felt Club app
  2. Go to Profile (tap your profile icon)
  3. Tap Settings (gear icon)
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your account profile and authentication data
  • Your name, email, and profile photo (if signed in with Google)
  • All game scores and match history
  • Player profiles you created
  • Any other data associated with your account in our systems

Note: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please ensure you want to proceed before submitting a request.

Data Retention After Deletion

Once your deletion request is processed, your data will be permanently removed from our active systems. Some data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being purged, but this data is not accessible and will be automatically deleted.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us:

Email: admin@thegreenfeltclub.com
