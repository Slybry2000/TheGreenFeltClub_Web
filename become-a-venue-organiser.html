INDEX / 07   VENUE FAQ

Common questions
from venue owners.

How much does it cost?

Standard is $3,990/year (or $399/month) — unlimited competitions and players, branded TV display, multi-comp rotation, priority support. Running more than one room? Multi-venue is $3,990/year for the first venue + $2,990/year each additional, with cross-venue leaderboards, white-label display, and manager rollups. Annual plans include a 30-day free trial. Right now there is also a Founding Venue offer: $799/year locked for 3 years — full Standard features, first 5 venues only — and once those are gone, Charter pricing at $2,388/year for venues 6–10. Cancel anytime.

What counts as one venue?

One physical address with pool tables. Multiple rooms or floors at the same address count as a single venue. Separate buildings each need their own subscription — that is where the Multi-venue plan kicks in.

How does the Founding Venue offer work?

$799/year for full Standard features, locked at that rate for three years. After year three, you keep a 20% loyalty discount for life. Annual prepay only, no trial. Includes direct Slack access to the founder. Capped at the first 5 venues, or 2026-09-30, whichever comes first. Venues 6–10 get Charter pricing — $2,388/year, the same 3-year lock and features.

Do players need to pay?

No. Players are always free. The venue subscription covers everything. Players just open the web app and play.

How long does setup take?

Most venues are live in under 10 minutes. Claim the venue, create a competition, share the join link, and open the TV display.

What do I need for the TV display?

Any screen with a web browser. Smart TV, Chromecast, Fire Stick, laptop connected to a monitor. Open the display URL and it auto-updates live.

What game types are supported?

8-ball and 9-ball with configurable rules, race lengths, and scoring modes. See the full competition guide for details.

Is this a league management system?

Not exactly. It is competition management, built for recurring venue nights, weekly tournaments, and house events. It handles scoring, standings, and player access. Full league season management is on the roadmap.

Do venues need approval?

Yes. We verify each venue to keep the platform trustworthy. Approval is usually same-day after you submit the claim form.

Can players join from a link?

Yes. Each competition has a join page URL and QR code. Players scan or tap to join. No app download needed.
