What “venue organiser” means

A venue competition organiser runs in-house events like weekly tournaments, league nights, rivalry ladders, or special 8-ball and 9-ball nights using organiser tools inside the app. Once your venue is approved, you can create competitions, manage participation, and share player join plus live display links from the same workflow.

Spelling note: organiser/organizer are the same role.

How to become a venue organiser

  1. 1

    Submit a venue claim request

    Use the web form to request venue access. Include any optional proof or notes that help verify the venue and the person requesting organiser access.

  2. 2

    Wait for approval

    Admin reviews the request and, if approved, enables organiser controls for that venue so competition tools are tied to the correct location.

  3. 3

    Create and run competitions

    After approval, create competitions such as 8-ball or 9-ball nights, choose the scoring mode, set a schedule window, and cap participants as needed.

  4. 4

    Share join + live display links

    Each approved competition can include a player join page for signups and a public live display page for TVs or monitors showing standings.

Why organisers use it

Cleaner night-of operations

Keep competition setup, player access, and public standings connected instead of juggling paper lists, group messages, and manual updates.

A better player experience

Players get a clear way to join, see the venue competition, and follow live progress without asking staff for every update.

Built for pool formats

The organiser path supports the same product direction as the player side: live 8-ball and 9-ball tracking, real records, and reusable competition structure.

Account-backed control

Organiser access is tied to the venue approval flow so support, control, and ongoing updates stay connected to the right people.

What you’ll need (recommended)

  • A consistent venue name used by players.
  • A staff contact (email) for questions during approval.
  • A planned competition format such as 8-ball, 9-ball, or a recurring house event.
  • A TV/monitor (optional) if you want a live standings display.

FAQ for venues

What kinds of competitions fit this organiser flow?

Recurring 8-ball and 9-ball nights, weekly tournaments, and house competitions are the clearest fit for the current organiser setup.

Do venues need approval before competitions can be run?

Yes. Venue approval is part of the organiser flow so competition controls are attached to the correct venue and contact.

Can players join from a link?

Yes. Approved competitions can expose player join pages so signups are easier to manage and share.

Can the venue show live standings on a screen?

Yes. Competitions can include a public live display page designed for TVs or monitors showing current standings.
