Venue FAQ

Common questions from venue owners and organisers before getting started.

How much does it cost?

Three tiers, billed annually with a 30-day free trial: Starter $500/year (1 competition, standard display), Pro $1,000/year (unlimited competitions, branded display, multi-comp rotation, priority support), Multi-venue $2,000/year (multiple venues, white-label display, manager rollups). Monthly billing is also available without a trial. Cancel anytime.

Do players need to pay?

No. Players are always free. The venue subscription covers everything — players just open the web app and play.

How long does setup take?

Most venues are live in under 10 minutes. Claim your venue, create a competition, share the join link, and open the TV display.

What do I need for the TV display?

Any screen with a web browser — smart TV, Chromecast, Fire Stick, laptop connected to a monitor. Open the display URL and it auto-updates live.

What game types are supported?

8-ball and 9-ball with configurable rules, race lengths, and scoring modes. See the full competition guide for details.

Is this a league management system?

Not exactly. It's competition management — built for recurring venue nights, weekly tournaments, and house events. It handles scoring, standings, and player access. Full league season management is on the roadmap.

Do venues need approval?

Yes. We verify each venue to keep the platform trustworthy. Approval is usually same-day after you submit the claim form.

Can players join from a link?

Yes. Each competition has a join page URL and QR code. Players scan or tap to join — no app download needed.
