INDEX / 07   VENUE FAQ

Common questions
from venue owners.

Is the leaderboard and content display really free?

Yes. An approved venue gets one live screen, its house leaderboard, up to three venue content slides, and the Green Felt Club house-content rotation for $0, with no credit card and no expiry date. Paid plans add organised competitions, advanced controls, branding, and more capacity.

What do the paid plans cost?

Standard is $3,990/year (or $399/month) for unlimited competitions and players, branded displays, multi-competition rotation, and priority support. Multi-venue starts at $3,990/year for the first venue plus $2,990/year each additional. A limited Founding Venue offer is $799/year, locked for three years; venues 6–10 receive Charter pricing at $2,388/year.

What counts as one venue?

One physical address with pool tables. Multiple rooms or floors at the same address count as a single venue. Separate buildings each need their own subscription — that is where the Multi-venue plan kicks in.

How does the Founding Venue offer work?

$799/year for full Standard features, locked at that rate for three years. After year three, you keep a 20% loyalty discount for life. Annual prepay only, no trial. Includes direct Slack access to the founder. Capped at the first 5 venues, or 2026-09-30, whichever comes first. Venues 6–10 get Charter pricing — $2,388/year, the same 3-year lock and features.

Do players need to pay?

No. Players are always free. They sign in, check in at the venue, and log games so the house leaderboard can update.

How long does setup take?

Claim the venue, open the private display link on your screen, and add your first content slide. Your players can then check in and start building the house leaderboard.

What do I need for the TV display?

A TV or monitor connected to a compatible web browser and a reliable internet connection. Open the display URL and leave it running; one active screen is included on the free plan.

What game types are supported?

8-ball and 9-ball with configurable rules, race lengths, and scoring modes. See the full competition guide for details.

Is this a league management system?

Not exactly. It is competition management, built for recurring venue nights, weekly tournaments, and house events. It handles scoring, standings, and player access. Full league season management is on the roadmap.

Do venues need approval?

Yes, but most new claims receive organiser access immediately and are reviewed afterward. If another account already controls the venue, the claim waits for a manual check.

Can players join from a link?

Yes. Each competition has a join page URL and QR code. Players scan or tap to join. No app download needed.
