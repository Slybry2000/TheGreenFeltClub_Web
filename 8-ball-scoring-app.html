INDEX / 04   COMMON QUESTIONS

Short answers,
fast.

Is this page specifically about 8-ball?

Yes. This page focuses on 8-ball scoring and live match tracking. The main site also covers 9-ball, stats, and venue competition tools.

Can I use it for casual groups?

Yes. It is built for players and groups who want to log real results without turning the night into admin work.

Does it support called pocket?

Yes. Ball-in-hand, call pocket, hard scratch on the 8. Team variant for 2-on-2. Plays the way your room plays.

Do players need accounts?

Account-backed profiles are the best way to keep long-term stats and synced history. Players are always free.
