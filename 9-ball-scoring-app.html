INDEX / 04   COMMON QUESTIONS

Short answers,
fast.

Is this only for 9-ball?

This page is focused on 9-ball search intent. The wider app also supports 8-ball, stats, and venue competition workflows.

Can casual players use it too?

Yes. It is suitable for casual groups who still want a reliable record of who won and how the matchup has gone over time.

Does it handle handicaps?

Yes. Built-in points-to-win tables for skill levels 1 through 9, push-out tracking, and automatic foul counting.

Where does history live?

Account-backed and cloud-synced. Sign in on any device, the history is right there.
